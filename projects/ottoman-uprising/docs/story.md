# OTTOMAN UPRISING - Storyline
## Historical Strategy Game - 1002 AD

---

# LOGLINE

A disgraced Armenian commander must penetrate deep into Turkish territory to rescue his captured people, confronting his former friend turned traitor, a brilliant enemy general, and the terrible cost of his own vengeful mission.

---

# SYNOPSIS

In the year 1002, the Armenian highlands burn under Turkish occupation. Three years after the destruction of his garrison and the capture of hundreds of his people—including his beloved Anahit and his younger brother Aram—Armen Vardanyan emerges from hiding with a desperate plan: to infiltrate the heart of Turkish power, free the prisoners, and strike a blow that will echo through history.

But Armen's mission is more complicated than simple rescue. His former friend and trusted ally Rundik betrayed the garrison to save his own life, and now serves as the Turks' intelligence officer. The brilliant Lehabib Kaya commands the occupation with calculated efficiency, while the ambitious Yilmaz hunts Armen with obsessive determination. At the summit of power sits Malikhor Chenani, an emperor who believes conquest is civilization.

Armen's journey takes him from the snowy peaks of the Armenian highlands through treacherous mountain passes, into enemy-held villages, and finally to the heart of Chenani's fortress. Along the way, he must make impossible choices: between personal vengeance and collective salvation, between his humanity and his mission, between the man he was and the man he is becoming.

The story explores themes of cultural survival, the cost of resistance, and the question of whether any victory justifies what war costs. There are no easy answers, no clean victories—only the difficult, painful work of trying to preserve something worth preserving in a world determined to destroy it.

---

# STRUCTURE

## Overview

The game follows a three-act structure, with each act containing multiple chapters. Player choices at key moments affect the narrative direction, character relationships, and available endings.

**Total Chapters:** 18
**Estimated Play Time:** 25-35 hours
**Key Decision Points:** 12 major choices, 40+ minor choices

---

# ACT I: DISCOVERY AND PREPARATION
## "The weight of three years"

### Theme
Armen confronts the cost of his failure and prepares for a mission that may be suicide. He must gather allies, intelligence, and supplies while evading Turkish patrols.

### Emotional Arc
Grief → Guilt → Determination → Hope

---

### Chapter 1: The Mountain's Shadow
**Location:** Armenian Highlands, abandoned monastery

#### Scene: The Hermit's Cave
Armen awakens in his mountain hideout, a converted cave system in the mountains above Lake Van. For three years, he has lived like an animal—hunting, hiding, planning. The opening montage shows his daily routine: checking traps, maintaining weapons, studying maps by candlelight.

**Flashback:** The fall of the garrison. Armen watches from a clifftop as Turkish forces overwhelm his position. He sees Anahit dragged away. He sees Rundik surrender. He sees his world burn. He could do nothing.

**Present Day:** Armen receives intelligence from a network of resistance fighters. A Turkish supply convoy is moving prisoners to a new location. Among them: Anahit, his brother Aram, and 200 other Armenians. If they reach the fortress of Chenani, they will be lost forever.

**Key Dialogue:**
> **Armen:** "Three years. Three years I've been planning this. Every blade of grass, every stone, every shadow—I know them all."
> **Tigran:** "And if it's not enough?"
> **Armen:** "Then we die. But we die fighting. Not hiding."

**Player Choice:** Armen can choose his approach:
- **Direct Action:** Lead an immediate ambush on the convoy
- **Patient Planning:** Spend more time gathering intelligence
- **Guerrilla Tactics:** Use the terrain to divide and isolate the convoy

---

### Chapter 2: The Weight of Memory
**Location:** Armenian Highlands, resistance camp

#### Scene: Council of the Damned
Armen convenes his small band of fighters: Tigran the scout, Davit the blacksmith, and a dozen other survivors. Each has lost something to the Turkish occupation. Each has their own reasons for fighting.

**Character Introductions:**
- **Tigran:** Young, eager, and naive. He has never been in real combat.
- **Davit:** Old, practical, and cynical. He has seen too many failed rebellions.
- **Mariam:** The healer, who questions the morality of continued resistance.
- **Father Gregory:** The historian, who reminds them of what they're fighting for.

**Key Dialogue:**
> **Davit:** "You want to attack a Turkish convoy with twenty men? This is madness."
> **Armen:** "We've been fighting madness with caution for three years. Where has it gotten us?"
> **Mariam:** "Perhaps the question isn't how to fight, but whether we should."
> **Armen:** "They have our people. They have our future. If we don't fight, we deserve to die."

**Player Choice:** Armen must convince or command his allies:
- **Inspire:** Appeal to their sense of duty and honor
- **Persuade:** Use logic and intelligence to make his case
- **Command:** Order them to follow, asserting his authority

---

### Chapter 3: The Price of Truth
**Location:** Armenian Highlands, abandoned village

#### Scene: The Survivors
Armen visits the ruins of his home village, now a ghost town. He discovers a small group of survivors hiding in the cellars: elderly men, women with young children, and a handful of wounded fighters.

Among them is Father Hovhannes, who has been conducting secret services and maintaining the community's spiritual life. He challenges Armen's militarism:

**Key Dialogue:**
> **Hovhannes:** "You speak of rescue, but you bring only war. What will be left of us when the fighting stops?"
> **Armen:** "Everything will be left. That's why we fight."
> **Hovhannes:** "And what if there's nothing left to save? What if we've become what we're fighting against?"

**Strategic Decision:** Armen must decide what to do with the survivors:
- **Leave Them:** Protect them by keeping them hidden
- **Recruit Them:** Add them to his forces
- **Evacuate Them:** Send them to safer territory
- **Use Them:** Employ them as scouts, cooks, or support

Each choice affects the final battle and multiple endings.

---

### Chapter 4: The Smith's Bargain
**Location:** Armenian Highlands, Davit's forge

#### Scene: Forging Weapons
Davit has been secretly maintaining a small forge, creating weapons and equipment for the resistance. Armen needs him to produce specialized equipment for the infiltration mission.

**Player Choice:** Armen can choose the focus of preparation:
- **Stealth Equipment:** Silent weapons, lockpicks, camouflage
- **Combat Equipment:** Better armor, stronger weapons, siege tools
- **Intelligence Tools:** Disguises, forged documents, communication equipment
- **Balanced Approach:** Moderate improvements across all categories

Each choice affects available tactics in later chapters.

---

### Chapter 5: The Road to Ruin
**Location:** Armenian Highlands, mountain pass

#### Scene: First Contact
Armen's small force moves through the mountains, avoiding Turkish patrols. They encounter a Turkish scouting party led by Deniz, the arrogant young Khudatin.

**First Combat Tutorial:** The player learns basic combat mechanics while facing a manageable threat.

**Key Dialogue:**
> **Deniz:** "Armenians. Rats in the mountains."
> **Armen:** "We are the mountains. You are merely guests."
> **Deniz:** "Guests who own this land now."
> **Armen:** "Ownership is not taken. It is remembered."

**Player Choice:** After defeating the scouts:
- **Kill Them:** Prevent them from reporting, but lose potential intelligence
- **Interrogate Them:** Extract information, then decide their fate
- **Release Them:** Send a message, but risk being tracked
- **Recruit One:** Turn a prisoner into an informant

---

### Chapter 6: The Healer's Warning
**Location:** Armenian Highlands, hidden camp

#### Scene: The Cost of War
Mariam treats wounded fighters and confronts Armen about the growing brutality of the resistance. A captured Turkish soldier is being interrogated by Davit, who is known for his harsh methods.

**Key Dialogue:**
> **Mariam:** "This is what you've become? Torturing prisoners?"
> **Armen:** "We need information. Time is running out."
> **Mariam:** "And when did we decide that some lives are worth less than others?"
> **Armen:** "When they took everything from us."
> **Mariam:** "Then they've already won."

**Player Choice:** Armen must resolve the situation:
- **Stop the Torture:** Maintain moral standards, lose intelligence
- **Allow It:** Get information, but damage relationships
- **Do It Himself:** Take responsibility, alienating allies
- **Find Another Way:** Use diplomacy or deception instead

---

### Chapter 7: The Betrayer's Shadow
**Location:** Armenian Highlands, crossroads

#### Scene: The First Encounter
Armen's force encounters evidence of Rundik's presence—a destroyed resistance outpost, prisoners taken. Among the dead: Tigran's younger brother.

**Key Dialogue:**
> **Tigran:** "Rundik did this. I know his methods."
> **Armen:** "He's tracking us."
> **Tigran:** "Then we hunt him."
> **Armen:** "No. He wants us to react. We move forward. We complete the mission."
> **Tigran:** "And my brother?"
> **Armen:** "We honor him by surviving."

**Player Choice:** Armen must manage Tigran's grief:
- **Offer Comfort:** Build relationship, but appear weak
- **Demand Discipline:** Maintain order, but risk alienation
- **Share the Grief:** Acknowledge loss while maintaining focus
- **Promise Vengeance:** Channel anger into determination

---

### Chapter 8: The Crossroads
**Location:** Armenian Highlands, mountain summit

#### Scene: The Plan
Armen reaches a vantage point overlooking Turkish territory. Below, the road to Chenani's fortress stretches into the distance. He must decide his approach.

**Player Choice:** Multiple routes are available:
- **The Northern Pass:** Shorter but heavily guarded
- **The Southern Valley:** Longer but less patrolled
- **The Underground Route:** Dangerous caves, but complete concealment
- **The River Path:** Fast, but exposed to archers

Each route has different challenges, allies, and consequences.

---

# ACT II: JOURNEY THROUGH ENEMY TERRITORY
## "The road to hell is paved with good intentions"

### Theme
Armen navigates the moral complexities of war, facing choices that test his values and force him to question his mission.

### Emotional Arc
Determination → Conflict → Doubt → Resolve

---

### Chapter 9: The Village of Ghosts
**Location:** Turkish-controlled village, Armenian quarter

#### Scene: The Occupation
Armen's force infiltrates a village under Turkish control. They witness the daily reality of occupation: Armenians forced to pay heavy taxes, forbidden from speaking their language, compelled to house Turkish soldiers.

**Key Encounter:** Armen meets an Armenian merchant who collaborates with the Turks in exchange for protection. The merchant provides intelligence but demands a terrible price: Armen must eliminate a rival who threatens his business.

**Player Choice:**
- **Agree:** Gain intelligence, but compromise principles
- **Refuse:** Maintain integrity, but lose the information
- **Negotiate:** Find a middle ground
- **Expose Him:** Reveal his crimes to the community

---

### Chapter 10: The Veteran's Code
**Location:** Turkish encampment, forest

#### Scene: The Duel
Armen encounters Tarkan, the Bardouin captain, during a patrol. Instead of calling for reinforcements, Tarkan challenges Armen to single combat—a test of skill and honor.

**Key Dialogue:**
> **Tarkan:** "I've been waiting for this. The Armenian who wouldn't die."
> **Armen:** "And I've been waiting for the man who destroyed my garrison."
> **Tarkan:** "I destroyed a military position. You should understand—you were a soldier."
> **Armen:** "I was a commander. You were a butcher."
> **Tarkan:** "Then let us see who is the better man."

**Boss Fight:** Tarkan is a formidable opponent, testing the player's combat skills.

**Aftermath:** If Armen defeats Tarkan:
- **Kill Him:** Remove a dangerous enemy, but lose potential intelligence
- **Spare Him:** Gain a grudging respect, but he may return
- **Recruit Him:** Attempt to turn him, high risk/high reward
- **Interrogate Him:** Extract information, then decide his fate

---

### Chapter 11: The Archer's Ambush
**Location:** Mountain pass, narrow gorge

#### Scene: The Trap
Armen's force walks into an ambush led by Seda, the Bardouin archer. She has been tracking them for days, using the terrain to her advantage.

**Combat Challenge:** The player must navigate a gauntlet of archer fire while protecting allies.

**Key Dialogue:**
> **Seda:** "You're predictable, Armenian. You always take the high ground."
> **Armen:** "And you always underestimate your enemy."
> **Seda:** "I never underestimate anyone. That's why I'm still alive."

**Player Choice:** After defeating Seda:
- **Kill Her:** Eliminate a skilled enemy
- **Spare Her:** She may provide information or return as an enemy
- **Use Her:** Force her to guide you through the territory

---

### Chapter 12: The Traitor's Game
**Location:** Abandoned fortress, intelligence post

#### Scene: The Confrontation
Armen discovers evidence of Rundik's operations: interrogation reports, surveillance records, and a list of Armenian prisoners who have been executed. Among the names: people Armen knew personally.

**Psychological Encounter:** Rundik leaves a message for Armen, demonstrating his intimate knowledge of Armenian habits and weaknesses.

**Key Dialogue (Recorded Message):**
> **Rundik:** "Hello, Armen. I know you're listening. I know you're angry. I also know you're afraid—afraid that you'll fail again, afraid that you've become what you hate. You're right to be afraid. I'm coming for you. Not because I want to kill you—I want to save you. I want to show you the truth: that everything you're fighting for is already dead. The only question is whether you'll die with it, or join the living."

**Player Choice:** Armen must decide how to respond:
- **Ignore It:** Maintain focus on the mission
- **Leave a Counter-Message:** Challenge Rundik directly
- **Set a Trap:** Use Rundik's obsession against him
- **Seek Understanding:** Try to comprehend Rundik's perspective

---

### Chapter 13: The River Crossing
**Location:** Major river, Turkish-controlled bridge

#### Scene: The Gateway
Armen's force must cross a heavily guarded bridge to reach Turkish heartland. Direct assault is impossible; they must find another way.

**Puzzle Sequence:** The player must solve environmental puzzles to cross the river:
- bribe guards
- steal a boat
- use underground tunnels
- wait for a distraction

**Key Encounter:** Armen meets a Turkish family who has lost their son to Chenani's conscription. They are willing to help in exchange for a promise to return their son if possible.

**Player Choice:**
- **Accept:** Gain allies, but add complexity to the mission
- **Refuse:** Maintain simplicity, but lose potential help
- **Lie:** Say yes, but plan to abandon them
- **Help Them:** Locate the son, adding time pressure

---

### Chapter 14: The General's Web
**Location:** Turkish administrative center, Lehabib Kaya's headquarters

#### Scene: The Intelligence
Armen infiltrates Kaya's intelligence network, discovering the full scope of the Turkish operation. He learns that the prisoners are being moved to a central processing center before being distributed throughout the empire.

**Key Revelation:** Kaya is not merely imprisoning Armenians—he is systematically destroying Armenian culture, forcing assimilation, and breaking community bonds. The prisoners are not just laborers; they are cultural hostages.

**Key Dialogue (Kaya, overheard):**
> **Kaya:** "You cannot conquer a people by killing them. You must conquer them by making them forget who they are. That is the true war—not of swords, but of memory."

**Player Choice:** Armen must decide how to use this intelligence:
- **Share It:** Inform allies, building a broader resistance
- **Keep It Secret:** Maintain strategic advantage
- **Use It Against Kaya:** Exploit weaknesses in his network
- **Destroy It:** Prevent the Turks from using the information

---

### Chapter 15: The Brother's Choice
**Location:** Prisoner convoy, night

#### Scene: The Rescue
Armen reaches the convoy and begins freeing prisoners. Among them: his brother Aram, now a young man he barely recognizes.

**Key Dialogue:**
> **Armen:** "Aram. Brother."
> **Aram:** "Who are you?"
> **Armen:** "I'm Armen. I've come to take you home."
> **Aram:** "Home? This is my home. These are my people."
> **Armen:** "They're your jailers."
> **Aram:** "They're my family. The family that took me in when you abandoned me."

**Emotional Confrontation:** Armen learns that Aram has been raised in a Turkish household, educated in Turkish ways, and taught to view his Armenian heritage as primitive. He resents Armen for leaving him, and struggles to reconcile his identities.

**Player Choice:** Armen must decide how to handle Aram:
- **Force Him:** Compel him to leave, damaging their relationship
- **Persuade Him:** Try to convince him of his Armenian identity
- **Accept Him:** Allow him to choose his own path
- **Leave Him:** Respect his decision, even if it means losing him

# ACT III: INFILTRATION AND FINAL CONFRONTATION
## "The measure of a man is what he does when he has nothing left to lose"

### Theme
Armen faces the ultimate test: completing his mission while preserving his humanity. Every choice has consequences that echo through the endings.

### Emotional Arc
Resolve → Conflict → Sacrifice → Transcendence

---

### Chapter 16: The Fortress of Shadows
**Location:** Chenani's fortress, approach

#### Scene: The Siege
Armen's force reaches the outskirts of Chenani's fortress. The final battle begins.

**Multi-Stage Battle:** The player must:
1. Breach the outer defenses
2. Navigate the courtyard
3. Reach the inner keep
4. Confront Chenani

**Key Choices:**
- **Approach:** Direct assault, stealth, or deception
- **Allies:** Who accompanies Armen in the final push
- **Sacrifice:** Some allies may die depending on player choices

---

### Chapter 17: The Traitor's Redemption
**Location:** Chenani's fortress, inner courtyard

#### Scene: The Confrontation
Armen encounters Rundik in the courtyard. This is their final meeting—the culmination of years of hatred, guilt, and loss.

**Key Dialogue:**
> **Rundik:** "You came. I knew you would."
> **Armen:** "You betrayed us. You betrayed everything we were."
> **Rundik:** "I survived. That's what matters."
> **Armen:** "Is it? Look at yourself. What have you become?"
> **Rundik:** "What you forced me to become. You and your honor, your principles—they're chains, Armen. I cut them."
> **Armen:** "And what's left?"
> **Rundik:** "Everything. Freedom. Power. The truth that loyalty is a myth."

**Final Confrontation:** The battle is both physical and psychological. Rundik knows Armen's weaknesses and exploits them mercilessly.

**Player Choice:** The most consequential decision in the game:
- **Kill Rundik:** End the betrayal, but lose any chance of redemption
- **Spare Him:** Offer mercy, but risk his return
- **Save Him:** Attempt to rescue him from Chenani's influence
- **Let Him Choose:** Allow Rundik to decide his own fate

---

### Chapter 18: The Emperor's Fall
**Location:** Chenani's throne room

#### Scene: The Final Confrontation
Armen reaches Chenani's throne room. The Emperor awaits, surrounded by his guard. This is not merely a battle—it is a debate, a negotiation, and a reckoning.

**Key Dialogue:**
> **Chenani:** "You've come a long way, Armenian. And for what? To kill one old man? To free a few hundred prisoners? The empire will continue. The world will continue."
> **Armen:** "Maybe. But I'll remember. They'll remember."
> **Chenani:** "Memory is the weakest weapon. I have armies, fortresses, laws. What do you have?"
> **Armen:** "I have the truth."
> **Chenani:** "Truth? What truth? That your people are weak? That they cannot defend themselves? That they need me to protect them from themselves?"
> **Armen:** "The truth that you've built your empire on bones. That your 'civilization' is just another word for destruction."
> **Chenani:** "All civilization is built on bones, Armenian. The question is whether the bones are worth the building."

**Boss Fight:** Chenani is not merely a warrior—he is a symbol. Defeating him requires understanding his weaknesses, both physical and ideological.

# ENDINGS

## The ending is determined by cumulative choices throughout the game, not just the final confrontation.

### Ending 1: The Martyr's Death
**Conditions:** Armen chooses violence throughout, kills Rundik, sacrifices allies
**Outcome:** Armen defeats Chenani but dies in the process. The prisoners are freed, but Armen never sees the results. The ending shows the survivors building a new community, with Armen's sacrifice as a founding myth. Dark, bittersweet, but hopeful.

### Ending 2: The Broken Hero
**Conditions:** Armen makes compromises, loses allies, kills Rundik
**Outcome:** Armen survives but is emotionally destroyed. The prisoners are freed, but Armen cannot connect with them. He wanders away, alone, into the mountains. The ending suggests that some wounds never heal.

### Ending 3: The Leader's Legacy
**Conditions:** Armen balances violence and mercy, preserves allies, spares Rundik
**Outcome:** Armen defeats Chenani and establishes a new resistance. He becomes a leader, not a martyr, building something that will last. The ending shows a new generation of Armenians, with Armen as their teacher and guide.

### Ending 4: The Reconciler
**Conditions:** Armen chooses mercy throughout, saves Rundik, prioritizes diplomacy
**Outcome:** Armen defeats Chenani through negotiation rather than violence. He establishes a peace agreement, sharing power with moderate Turkish elements. Rundik, redeemed, becomes an ambassador. The ending suggests that coexistence is possible, if difficult.

### Ending 5: The Exile
**Conditions:** Armen fails to gather enough support, loses key allies
**Outcome:** Armen escapes with a small group of prisoners but fails to defeat Chenani. The empire continues, but the resistance survives in hiding. The ending shows Armen training a new generation, waiting for the right moment to strike again. Hopeful but uncertain.

### Ending 6: The Dark Victory
**Conditions:** Armen becomes ruthless, sacrifices everyone, kills Chenani brutally
**Outcome:** Armen defeats Chenani and takes control of the fortress. He becomes the new tyrant, using Chenani's methods against his enemies. The ending shows Armen on Chenani's throne, having become what he fought against. Tragic, cautionary.

# KEY PLOT TWISTS

## Twist 1: The Brother's Choice (Chapter 15)
Aram refuses to leave with Armen, choosing his Turkish family over his Armenian heritage. This forces Armen to confront the true cost of conquest—not just death, but cultural erasure.

## Twist 2: The Healer's Secret (Chapter 12)
Mariam reveals that she has been in contact with moderate Turkish elements, working toward a negotiated peace. Armen must decide whether to support or condemn her efforts.

## Twist 3: The Traitor's Motivation (Chapter 17)
Rundik reveals that he betrayed the garrison not just to save his life, but because Armen's "honor" and "principles" were destroying the community. His betrayal was an act of desperation, not malice.

## Twist 4: The Emperor's Vision (Chapter 18)
Chenani reveals that he has been planning to retire, to pass power to a council that includes Armenian representatives. His conquest was not just destruction—it was preparation for a new order. This complicates the moral calculus of his defeat.

## Twist 5: The Final Choice (Chapter 18)
After defeating Chenani, Armen discovers that the prisoners have already been freed by Anahit and the resistance network. The final battle was unnecessary—Armen's personal vendetta nearly cost him everything. This forces him to confront the true motivation behind his mission.

RUNDIK: (laughing bitterly) Right? You let your people die because you were too proud to retreat. You let me be captured because you trusted too easily. And now you come here, killing my soldiers, destroying my work, and you think you're right?

## Sample 3: Armen and Chenani Debate

[SCENE: Throne room]

Chenani sits on his throne, unarmed. His guards lie defeated around him.

CHENANI: You've won, Armenian. Are you satisfied?

ARMEN: I'll be satisfied when my people are free.

CHENANI: Free? Free to do what? Fight each other? Rebuild the same petty kingdoms that brought nothing but war? I offered them peace. Order. Progress.

ARMEN: And your "order" is just another word for oppression.

CHENANI: (standing) Everything is worth dying for. The question is whether they're worth killing for.

[Beat.]

CHENANI: You've killed many of my men today. Were they worth dying for? Were they worth killing?

ARMEN: (hesitating) I don't know.

CHENANI: (nodding) That's the beginning of wisdom. The end is knowing that there's no answer.

[He extends his hand.]

CHENANI: Kill me, and the empire continues. Spare me, and perhaps we can build something together. What will you do, Armenian?

[CHOICE: Kill Chenani / Spare him / Negotiate]

# NARRATIVE MECHANICS

## Branching Points
The game tracks player choices through a hidden "moral compass" system:
- **Compassion vs. Ruthlessness:** How Armen treats enemies and civilians
- **Justice vs. Vengeance:** Whether Armen seeks retribution or restoration
- **Leadership vs. Independence:** How Armen balances personal mission with collective good
- **Faith vs. Pragmatism:** Whether Armen follows principles or adapts to circumstances

## Character Relationships
Key relationships evolve based on player choices:
- **Anahit:** Trust, respect, love (or its absence)
- **Tigran:** Mentorship, protection, legacy
- **Mariam:** Moral guidance, challenge, support
- **Rundik:** Hatred, understanding, redemption (or damnation)
- **Chenani:** Ideological conflict, respect, transcendence

## Consequence System
Choices ripple through the narrative:
- Early decisions affect available allies in the final battle
- Treatment of prisoners affects information available later
- Sacrifices made in early chapters affect the endings available
- The player cannot achieve everything—some goals require sacrificing others

# THEMATIC SUMMARY

## Core Themes

### The Cost of Resistance
Every victory has a price. Armen must decide what he's willing to pay—and whether the price is worth the result.

### Cultural Identity Under Pressure
What does it mean to be Armenian when everything Armenian is being destroyed? The game explores how culture survives through language, memory, and community.

### Moral Ambiguity in War
There are no clear good guys or bad guys. Every character has reasons for their actions, and every action has consequences.

### The Weight of Leadership
Armen must balance his personal desires with his responsibilities to others. The game asks: What do we owe each other?

### Redemption and Forgiveness
Can Rundik be saved? Can Armen forgive? Can enemies become allies? The game explores whether redemption is possible in a world built on violence.
