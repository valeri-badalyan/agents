# OTTOMAN UPRISING - Game Mechanics
## Historical Strategy Game - 1002 AD

---

# OVERVIEW

This document outlines the core gameplay mechanics for Ottoman Uprising, a historical strategy game that combines stealth, combat, resource management, and narrative-driven choices. Every mechanic serves the story's themes: the cost of war, cultural survival, and moral ambiguity.

**Core Pillars:**
1. **Meaningful Choice:** Every decision has consequences that ripple through the narrative
2. **Asymmetric Warfare:** David vs. Goliath dynamics—fewer resources, smarter tactics
3. **Cultural Identity:** Mechanics that reinforce the theme of cultural preservation
4. **Emotional Impact:** Systems that create tension, relief, sacrifice, and triumph

---

# COMBAT SYSTEM

## Philosophy
Combat in Ottoman Uprising is dangerous, consequential, and emotionally weighted. Every fight has a cost—in resources, health, morale, and narrative consequences. Players cannot simply kill their way to victory; they must choose their battles carefully.

---

## Melee Combat

### Basic Mechanics
- **Real-time with tactical pause:** Players can pause combat to issue orders or assess the situation
- **Stamina-based:** Attacks consume stamina; overcommitting leaves Armen vulnerable
- **Positioning matters:** Flanking, high ground, and terrain affect damage and defense
- **Weapon types:** Each weapon has unique movesets, strengths, and weaknesses

### Weapon Categories

#### Armenian Saber
- **Style:** Fast, precise, fluid
- **Strengths:** High attack speed, good for parrying, effective against unarmored targets
- **Weaknesses:** Low damage per hit, ineffective against heavy armor
- **Narrative Tie:** Represents Armenian martial tradition; Armen's father's weapon
- **Special Move:** *Vardanyan Strike* — A rapid three-hit combo that exploits enemy openings

#### Turkish Kilij
- **Style:** Heavy, powerful, brutal
- **Strengths:** High damage, effective against armor, wide arc attacks
- **Weaknesses:** Slow, high stamina cost, vulnerable to faster opponents
- **Narrative Tie:** Captured from enemies; represents the tools of oppression turned against them
- **Special Move:** *Reclaimer's Fury* — A devastating overhead strike that can break guard

#### Short Sword
- **Style:** Balanced, versatile
- **Strengths:** Moderate speed and damage, good for both offense and defense
- **Weaknesses:** No outstanding strengths; jack of all trades
- **Narrative Tie:** Practical weapon for infiltration; doesn't attract attention
- **Special Move:** *Shadow Strike* — A quick thrust from concealment

#### Daggers
- **Style:** Stealth, assassination
- **Strengths:** Fastest attacks, bonus damage from stealth, silent kills
- **Weaknesses:** Short range, low damage in open combat
- **Narrative Tie:** Tools of the shadows; necessary for infiltration
- **Special Move:** *Silent Reaper* — Instant kill from behind if undetected

### Combat Stances

#### Aggressive Stance
- **Effect:** +25% damage, -25% defense
- **Best for:** Quick kills, overwhelming weaker enemies
- **Risk:** Leaves Armen vulnerable to counterattacks
- **Narrative Reflection:** Represents Armen's rage and impatience

#### Defensive Stance
- **Effect:** +25% defense, -25% damage
- **Best for:** Surviving against multiple enemies, buying time
- **Risk:** Prolongs combat, drains stamina
- **Narrative Reflection:** Represents Armen's discipline and caution

#### Balanced Stance
- **Effect:** Normal damage and defense
- **Best for:** General combat situations
- **Risk:** No particular advantage or disadvantage
- **Narrative Reflection:** Represents Armen's training and experience

#### Stealth Stance
- **Effect:** +50% stealth damage, -50% open combat damage
- **Best for:** Infiltration, assassination, ambush
- **Risk:** Useless if detected
- **Narrative Reflection:** Represents Armen's guerrilla training

### Parry and Riposte System
- **Timing-based:** Perfect timing on block creates riposte opportunity
- **Risk-reward:** Failed parry takes extra damage
- **Enemy variety:** Different enemies have different attack patterns to learn
- **Skill progression:** Parry window increases with practice and upgrades

### Environmental Combat
- **Terrain:** Mud slows movement, snow muffles sound, rocks provide cover
- **Verticality:** High ground gives damage and visibility bonuses
- **Destructibles:** Breakable objects can be used as weapons or distractions
- **Traps:** Environmental hazards can be triggered or avoided

## Ranged Combat

### Bow Mechanics
- **Draw time:** Bows require time to draw fully; rushed shots are inaccurate
- **Stamina cost:** Holding a drawn bow drains stamina
- **Ammunition:** Limited arrows; must be recovered or crafted
- **Wind effect:** Environmental conditions affect arrow trajectory

### Bow Types

#### Short Bow
- **Range:** Medium
- **Damage:** Low
- **Draw Time:** Fast
- **Use:** Skirmishing, hunting, signaling
- **Narrative Tie:** Practical weapon for a guerrilla fighter

#### Composite Bow
- **Range:** Long
- **Damage:** Medium
- **Draw Time:** Medium
- **Use:** Sniping, ambush
- **Narrative Tie:** Turkish-style bow; Armen learned to use enemy weapons

#### Crossbow
- **Range:** Long
- **Damage:** High
- **Draw Time:** Very Slow
- **Use:** Siege defense, heavy targets
- **Narrative Tie:** Rare, powerful; represents technological advantage

### Thrown Weapons
- **Daggers:** Short range, high damage, silent
- **Rocks:** Medium range, low damage, available everywhere
- **Smoke Bombs:** Creates concealment for escape or approach
- **Fire Bombs:** Area denial, damages structures

## Stealth System

### Core Mechanics
- **Visibility system:** Armen's visibility is affected by lighting, movement, and cover
- **Sound system:** Different surfaces make different amounts of noise
- **Detection states:** Unaware → Suspicious → Alerted → Combat
- **Line of sight:** Enemies must actually see Armen to detect him

### Stealth States

#### Undetected
- **Effect:** Enemies are unaware of Armen's presence
- **Bonus:** +100% stealth damage, ability to perform silent kills
- **Visual:** Green indicator on HUD
- **Sound:** Minimal; can move freely

#### Suspicious
- **Effect:** Enemies have noticed something unusual but haven't identified Armen
- **Bonus:** +50% stealth damage if Armen remains unseen
- **Visual:** Yellow indicator on HUD
- **Sound:** Enemies will investigate; Armen must stay hidden or act natural

#### Alerted
- **Effect:** Enemies have identified Armen as a threat
- **Bonus:** No stealth bonuses; combat begins
- **Visual:** Red indicator on HUD
- **Sound:** Enemies will attack on sight

#### Combat
- **Effect:** Full combat engagement
- **Bonus:** None
- **Visual:** Red flashing indicator
- **Sound:** All enemies in area are alerted

### Stealth Actions

#### Silent Kill
- **Requirement:** Undetected, behind enemy
- **Effect:** Instant kill; no alert raised
- **Risk:** If the body is discovered, enemies become suspicious
- **Narrative Tie:** Necessary for infiltration; morally ambiguous

#### Knockout
- **Requirement:** Undetected, behind enemy
- **Effect:** Temporarily incapacitates enemy; can be discovered
- **Risk:** Enemy may wake up; must be restrained or hidden
- **Narrative Tie:** More merciful than killing; preserves moral standing

#### Distraction
- **Requirement:** Thrown object or environmental trigger
- **Effect:** Draws enemy attention to a specific location
- **Risk:** Enemies may investigate the source
- **Narrative Tie:** Represents tactical thinking over brute force

#### Disguise
- **Requirement:** Acquired disguise item
- **Effect:** Allows Armen to move freely in restricted areas
- **Risk:** May be discovered by enemies who know the person being impersonated
- **Narrative Tie:** Represents cultural knowledge and adaptability

### Stealth Upgrades

#### Night Vision
- **Effect:** Improved visibility in dark areas
- **Requirement:** Herbal preparation from Mariam
- **Cost:** Consumable resource

#### Muffled Steps
- **Effect:** Reduced noise from movement
- **Requirement:** Special footwear from Davit
- **Cost:** Equipment slot

#### Shadow Cloak
- **Effect:** Improved concealment in shadows
- **Requirement:** Dark clothing from trade
- **Cost:** Reduced armor protection

#### Eagle Eye
- **Effect:** Extended vision range for scouting
- **Requirement:** Training with Tigran
- **Cost:** Skill point investment

## Tactical Pause System

### Overview
Players can pause combat at any time to issue orders, assess the situation, and plan their next move. This represents Armen's tactical mind and allows for strategic decision-making.

### Commands Available During Pause

#### Movement Orders
- **Move To:** Direct Armen to a specific location
- **Flank:** Move to enemy's side or rear
- **Retreat:** Move to safety
- **Advance:** Move toward objective

#### Combat Orders
- **Attack Target:** Focus fire on specific enemy
- **Defend Position:** Hold current position
- **Use Item:** Apply medicine, use special equipment
- **Switch Weapon:** Change to appropriate weapon for situation

#### Ally Orders
- **Follow:** Allies follow Armen's lead
- **Hold:** Allies maintain position
- **Attack:** Allies engage enemies
- **Retreat:** Allies fall back to safety

### Tactical Depth
- **Enemy patterns:** Each enemy type has predictable behaviors that can be exploited
- **Synergy:** Combining different ally abilities creates powerful combinations
- **Environmental awareness:** Using terrain and objects to advantage
- **Resource management:** Deciding when to use limited resources

# RESOURCE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

## Philosophy
Resources in Ottoman Uprising are scarce, meaningful, and tied to narrative choices. The player cannot hoard everything—they must make difficult decisions about what to prioritize. This reinforces the theme of scarcity and the cost of war.

---

## Health System

### Physical Health
- **Health Points:** Armen has a health bar that depletes when taking damage
- **Injury Types:** Different attacks cause different injuries with varying effects
- **Healing:** Requires medical supplies or rest at safe locations
- **Permanent Damage:** Some injuries leave lasting effects

### Injury Types

#### Minor Wounds
- **Effect:** Minor health loss, slight stamina reduction
- **Healing:** Self-healing over time, basic medical supplies
- **Duration:** Temporary

#### Serious Wounds
- **Effect:** Significant health loss, major stamina reduction, movement penalty
- **Healing:** Requires medical supplies or healer assistance
- **Duration:** Long-term; may require rest

#### Critical Wounds
- **Effect:** Severe health loss, inability to fight, risk of death
- **Healing:** Requires immediate medical attention
- **Duration:** Permanent if untreated

### Mental Health
- **Morale System:** Armen's mental state affects gameplay
- **Trauma:** Witnessing or committing atrocities affects morale
- **Recovery:** Rest, social interaction, and purpose restore morale
- **Effects:** Low morale reduces combat effectiveness and decision-making

### Morale Indicators
- **High Morale:** +10% combat effectiveness, better dialogue options
- **Normal Morale:** Standard performance
- **Low Morale:** -10% combat effectiveness, worse dialogue options
- **Critical Morale:** Risk of breakdown; Armen may refuse orders

---

## Equipment System

### Weapon Maintenance
- **Durability:** Weapons degrade with use
- **Repair:** Requires materials and Davit's expertise
- **Degradation Effects:** Reduced damage, slower speed, risk of breaking
- **Narrative Tie:** Represents the constant struggle to maintain equipment in the field

### Armor System
- **Layers:** Light, medium, heavy armor with different properties
- **Coverage:** Different armor protects different body parts
- **Stealth Penalty:** Heavier armor makes stealth more difficult
- **Stamina Cost:** Heavier armor drains stamina faster

### Equipment Slots
- **Primary Weapon:** Main combat weapon
- **Secondary Weapon:** Backup or specialized weapon
- **Ranged Weapon:** Bow or crossbow
- **Armor:** Body protection
- **Accessory:** Special equipment (lockpicks, disguises, etc.)
- **Consumables:** Medical supplies, tools, thrown weapons

---

## Supply System

### Food and Water
- **Hunger:** Armen must eat regularly or suffer penalties
- **Thirst:** Armen must drink regularly or suffer penalties
- **Quality:** Better food provides better bonuses
- **Sourcing:** Must be bought, hunted, or found

### Medical Supplies
- **Herbs:** Basic healing materials found in nature
- **Medicine:** Crafted by Mariam from herbs and other ingredients
- **Bandages:** Stop bleeding, prevent infection
- **Special Preparations:** Advanced medicines with unique effects

### Crafting Materials
- **Wood:** For weapons, tools, and structures
- **Metal:** For weapons and armor
- **Cloth:** For clothing, bandages, and camouflage
- **Leather:** For armor and equipment

### Currency
- **Barter System:** No standardized currency; trade goods for goods
- **Value Varies:** Different items are valued differently in different locations
- **Black Market:** Rare items available at premium prices
- **Bribery:** Can be used to avoid conflict or gain access

- **Recruitment:** Allies join based on player choices and reputation
- **Retention:** Allies stay based on treatment and shared purpose
- **Development:** Allies improve through use and investment
- **Loss:** Allies can die or leave based on player decisions

- **Heavy Combat:** Deals high damage in melee

#### Mariam

- **Morale:** Improves ally performance and dialogue options
- **Diplomacy:** Negotiates with neutral parties
- **Knowledge:** Provides cultural and historical information

#### Anahit
- **Leadership:** Improves all ally performance
- **Intelligence:** Gathers information from prisoners and civilians
- **Organization:** Manages supplies and logistics
- **Inspiration:** Provides narrative bonuses in key moments

### Ally Relationships
- **Trust:** Built through consistent, ethical behavior
- **Respect:** Earned through competence and leadership
- **Friendship:** Developed through shared experience and support
- **Romance:** Possible with certain allies; affects narrative and gameplay

### Relationship Mechanics
- **Dialogues:** Regular conversations build relationships
- **Gifts:** Giving appropriate items improves relationships
- **Shared Experience:** Fighting together builds bonds
- **Conflict:** Disagreements can damage relationships
- **Resolution:** Conflicts can be resolved through dialogue or compromise

# MORAL CHOICE SYSTEM

## Philosophy
Moral choices in Ottoman Uprising are not about good vs. evil—they're about competing goods and necessary evils. Every choice has legitimate arguments on both sides, and the player must decide what kind of leader Armen will become.

---

## Choice Categories

### Compassion vs. Ruthlessness
**Focus:** How Armen treats enemies and civilians

#### Compassionate Choices
- Spare surrendered enemies
- Protect civilians at personal risk
- Prioritize rescue over revenge
- Seek diplomatic solutions

**Consequences:**
- Allies respect Armen's humanity
- Enemies may surrender more readily
- Armen maintains moral clarity
- Some objectives become more difficult

#### Ruthless Choices
- Kill surrendered enemies
- Use civilians as shields or bait
- Prioritize mission over individual lives
- Use violence as first resort

**Consequences:**
- Enemies fight to the death
- Allies may question Armen's leadership
- Armen becomes more effective in combat
- Some narrative paths are closed

### Justice vs. Vengeance
**Focus:** Whether Armen seeks retribution or restoration

#### Justice Choices
- Hold enemies accountable for specific crimes
- Seek proportional punishment
- Protect due process and fairness
- Focus on systemic change

**Consequences:**
- Creates lasting institutional change
- May leave some crimes unpunished
- Builds legitimacy for new order
- Requires patience and restraint

#### Vengeance Choices
- Punish all enemies regardless of guilt
- Seek personal satisfaction
- Make examples of defeated foes
- Focus on immediate retribution

**Consequences:**
- Provides emotional satisfaction
- May create martyrs or future enemies
- Undermines long-term goals
- Corrodes Armen's soul

### Leadership vs. Independence
**Focus:** How Armen balances personal mission with collective good

#### Leadership Choices
- Consult allies before major decisions
- Delegate tasks based on strengths
- Take responsibility for failures
- Share credit for successes

**Consequences:**
- Builds stronger alliances
- Slower decision-making
- Better outcomes in group situations
- Requires trust in others

#### Independence Choices
- Make decisions unilaterally
- Handle critical tasks personally
- Take full credit for success
- Blame others for failure

**Consequences:**
- Faster execution
- Better individual performance
- Alienates potential allies
- May miss important perspectives

### Faith vs. Pragmatism
**Focus:** Whether Armen follows principles or adapts to circumstances

#### Faith Choices
- Maintain moral principles regardless of cost
- Honor agreements even when inconvenient
- Trust in divine providence
- Preserve cultural traditions

**Consequences:**
- Maintains moral integrity
- May lead to defeat
- Inspires others through example
- Creates binding commitments

#### Pragmatism Choices
- Adapt principles to circumstances
- Break agreements when necessary
- Trust in personal judgment
- Abandon traditions that hinder survival

**Consequences:**
- Increases chances of success
- May compromise moral standing
- Allows flexibility and adaptation
- May lose cultural identity

### Anti-Grinding Philosophy
- **Experience Caps:** Diminishing returns from repeated actions

### Specialization Encouragement
- **Diminishing Returns:** Early points in a skill are most effective
- **Synergy Bonuses:** Combining related skills provides bonuses
- **Role Definition:** Different builds create different playstyles
- **No Perfect Build:** Every build has strengths and weaknesses

### Narrative Integration
- **Character Growth:** Progression reflects Armen's development

---

- **Risk:** Fast travel may trigger random encounters

### Local Maps
- **Detail:** Smaller-scale maps of specific locations
- **Markers:** Points of interest marked as discovered
- **Compass:** Directional indicator for objectives
- **Legend:** Explains map symbols

### Mini-Map
- **HUD Element:** Always visible during gameplay
- **Enemy Indicators:** Shows detected enemies
- **Ally Indicators:** Shows ally positions
- **Objective Indicators:** Shows current objective location

- **Stashes:** Hidden supply caches throughout the world

- **Purchases:** Items bought from merchants
- **Crafting:** Items created from gathered materials

### Secret Areas
- **Hidden Passages:** Secret tunnels and doors
- **Alternate Routes:** Hidden paths to objectives
- **Treasure Rooms:** Chambers containing valuable rewards

### Environmental Clues
- **Visual Indicators:** Subtle hints about hidden content

# DIFFICULTY SYSTEM

## Philosophy
Difficulty in Ottoman Uprising is not just about combat challenge—it's about the weight of consequences, the scarcity of resources, and the pressure of time.

---

## Difficulty Levels

### Story Mode
- **Focus:** Narrative experience over challenge
- **Combat:** Reduced damage taken, increased damage dealt
- **Resources:** Abundant supplies, easy crafting
- **Stealth:** Forgiving detection, easy escapes
- **Dialogue:** Clearer success indicators
- **Best For:** Players who want to experience the story without frustration

### Normal Mode
- **Focus:** Balanced challenge and narrative
- **Combat:** Standard damage values
- **Resources:** Moderate supplies, standard crafting
- **Stealth:** Standard detection, moderate difficulty
- **Dialogue:** Standard success rates
- **Best For:** Players who want a balanced experience

### Hard Mode
- **Focus:** Challenging but fair gameplay
- **Combat:** Increased damage taken, reduced damage dealt
- **Resources:** Scarce supplies, difficult crafting
- **Stealth:** Strict detection, difficult escapes
- **Dialogue:** Lower success rates, higher consequences
- **Best For:** Players who want a challenging experience

### Veteran Mode
- **Focus:** Extreme challenge for experienced players
- **Combat:** High damage taken, low damage dealt
- **Resources:** Very scarce supplies, very difficult crafting
- **Stealth:** Very strict detection, very difficult escapes
- **Dialogue:** Very low success rates, severe consequences
- **Best For:** Players who want maximum challenge

### Iron Mode
- **Focus:** Permadeath with no saves
- **Combat:** One death ends the game
- **Resources:** Standard resources but no reloads
- **Stealth:** Standard stealth but no second chances
- **Dialogue:** Standard dialogue but permanent consequences
- **Best For:** Players who want the ultimate challenge

---

## Dynamic Difficulty

### Adaptive Systems
- **Enemy Scaling:** Enemies become more difficult as Armen becomes stronger
- **Resource Scaling:** Resources become more scarce as Armen becomes more efficient
- **Stealth Scaling:** Detection becomes more strict as Armen becomes more skilled
- **Dialogue Scaling:** Consequences become more severe as Armen becomes more influential

### Player Assistance
- **Hints:** Optional hints for difficult sections
- **Recaps:** Story recaps after long breaks
- **Objectives:** Clear objective reminders
- **Map Assistance:** Additional map markers for lost players
