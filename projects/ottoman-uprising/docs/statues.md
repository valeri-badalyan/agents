# OTTOMAN UPRISING - Statue Spawn System
## Spawn Points in Cities and Key Locations

---

# OVERVIEW

Statues serve as spawn points throughout the game world. Each statue represents a historical Armenian figure or deity, providing both a gameplay mechanic (respawn point) and cultural/historical context. Players discover statues by exploring, and once activated, can respawn at that location upon death.

**Total Statues:** 15
**Categories:** Historical Figures, Deities, Ancestors, Unknown

---

# STATUE CATEGORIES

## Historical Figures
Statues depicting real or legendary Armenian historical figures.

## Deities
Statues of ancient Armenian gods and mythological beings.

## Ancestors
Memorial statues honoring unnamed ancestors and fallen warriors.

## Unknown
Mysterious statues of unknown origin, adding atmosphere and mystery.

# ARMENIAN HIGHLANDS

## Statue 1: Tigranes the Great
**Location:** Armen's Hideout (Starting Area)
**Type:** Historical Figure
**Description:** A majestic bronze statue of Tigranes the Great, the greatest Armenian king, holding a sword and scepter.

### Visual Design
- **Material:** Bronze with green patina
- **Height:** 3 meters
- **Pose:** Standing, sword raised, looking toward the horizon
- **Base:** Stone pedestal with ancient Armenian inscriptions
- **Lighting:** Lit by torches at night, glowing faintly

### Spawn Effect
- **Activation:** Touch the statue's base
- **Visual:** Golden light emanates from the statue
- **Sound:** Ancient Armenian chant
- **Message:** "The spirit of Tigranes guides you"

### Gameplay Function
- **Respawn Point:** Player respawns here upon death
- **Bonus:** +10% attack damage for 60 seconds after respawn
- **Lore:** Unlocks historical text about Tigranes the Great

# SPAWN SYSTEM MECHANICS

## Discovery
- Statues are hidden throughout the world
- Players must find and activate them
- Each statue has a unique activation method
- Discovery is rewarded with lore and bonuses

## Activation
- Each statue requires a different interaction:
  - Touch
  - Pray
  - Leave offering
  - Perform action
  - Solve puzzle

## Respawn
- Upon death, player respawns at last activated statue
- Respawn includes temporary bonus effect
- Bonuses encourage strategic statue usage
- Some statues provide unique advantages

## Progression
- Early statues provide basic bonuses
- Later statues provide stronger bonuses
- Hidden statues provide unique bonuses
- Final statue provides ultimate bonus
