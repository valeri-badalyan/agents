# Visual Style Guide - Ottoman Uprising (Hero of Armenia)

## Art Direction Overview

**Game Style:** Historical Realism with Painterly Touches
**Visual Philosophy:** Authentic medieval aesthetics combined with dramatic storytelling through visual contrast between light and shadow, freedom and oppression.

---

## Mood and Atmosphere Guide

### Overall Tone
**Dramatic Tension** - Constant contrast between hope and despair, freedom and oppression
**Historical Authenticity** - Grounded, realistic medieval setting
**Emotional Weight** - Every visual element tells part of the story

### Key Visual Themes
1. **Freedom vs. Oppression** - Open highlands vs. fortified cities
2. **Light vs. Darkness** - Day operations vs. night stealth
3. **Nature vs. Civilization** - Organic landscapes vs. man-made structures
4. **Ancient vs. New** - Timeless mountains vs. changing borders

### Atmospheric Elements
- **Weather Systems:** Clear skies for hope, storms for conflict, fog for mystery
- **Time Progression:** Dawn (new beginnings), Day (action), Dusk (uncertainty), Night (stealth)
- **Environmental Storytelling:** Ruined villages, abandoned fortresses, living cities

## Lighting Styles

### Daytime Lighting
**Morning (Dawn):**
- Warm golden light from east
- Long shadows creating dramatic silhouettes
- Soft ambient occlusion
- Mist in valleys

**Midday:**
- Harsh overhead sunlight
- Strong contrast between light and shadow
- Clear visibility for tactical planning
- Heat haze effects in desert areas

**Afternoon:**
- Warmer, orange-tinted light
- Longer shadows for dramatic composition
- Softer contrast than midday
- Dust particles visible in light beams

### Nighttime Lighting
**Moonlight:**
- Cool blue-silver illumination
- Strong directional shadows
- Limited visibility radius
- Enhanced stealth mechanics visibility

**Torchlight:**
- Warm orange flickering light
- Dynamic shadow movement
- Limited radius creating tension
- Reflections on metallic surfaces

**Firelight:**
- Intense warm glow
- Particle effects for embers
- Dancing shadows on walls
- Camp/fortress ambiance

### Special Lighting Effects
**Religious/Icon Lighting:**
- Divine golden glow for icons
- Soft radiance in churches
- Mystical purple for secret rituals

**Combat Lighting:**
- Dynamic flashes from weapons
- Blood red highlights during violence
- Sparks from metal impacts
- Smoke effects obscuring vision

### Location-Based Lighting
**Armenian Highlands:**
- Open, natural lighting
- Soft shadows from mountains
- Clear atmospheric perspective
- Seasonal variations (spring green, autumn gold)

**Border Zones:**
- Harsh, dramatic lighting
- Strong contrasts representing conflict
- Smoke and dust effects
- Guard tower spotlights

**Turkish Cities:**
- Mixed natural and artificial light
- Bazaar warmth vs. palace austerity
- Night market lanterns
- Mosque illumination

**Chenani's Fortress:**
- Dark, foreboding atmosphere
- Minimal light sources
- Deep shadows for stealth
- Dramatic backlighting for boss encounters

## Reference Art Style

### Primary Style: Historical Realism with Painterly Touches
**Influences:**
- Medieval manuscript illuminations
- Renaissance historical paintings
- Modern historical concept art
- Armenian miniature painting traditions

### Character Art
**Style:** Semi-realistic with clear silhouettes
**Details:** Authentic period clothing and armor
**Expressions:** Subtle, period-appropriate emotions
**Proportions:** Realistic human proportions

### Environment Art
**Style:** Painterly realism with atmospheric perspective
**Details:** Historical architectural accuracy
**Textures:** Hand-painted with photographic references
**Composition:** Rule of thirds, leading lines, focal points

### UI Art
**Style:** Medieval manuscript inspired with modern clarity
**Elements:** Ornate borders, historical typography
**Icons:** Pictographic with cultural symbols
**Animations:** Subtle, purposeful movements

### Reference Artists/Works
- **Historical:** Armenian illuminated manuscripts, Ottoman miniatures
- **Modern:** Historical concept art from AAA strategy games
- **Traditional:** Classical landscape painters (for environmental composition)
- **Cultural:** Armenian and Turkish traditional art motifs

## Visual Hierarchy

### Primary Elements
1. **Characters** - Clear, detailed, emotionally expressive
2. **Interactive Objects** - Highlighted, obvious, inviting
3. **UI Elements** - Clean, readable, non-intrusive

### Secondary Elements
1. **Environment Details** - Atmospheric, contextual, immersive
2. **Background Characters** - Simplified but period-appropriate
3. **Weather Effects** - Subtle, mood-enhancing

### Tertiary Elements
1. **Distant Scenery** - Impressionistic, atmospheric perspective
2. **Particle Effects** - Atmospheric, non-distracting
3. **Ambient Details** - Life-giving but not essential

---

## Artistic Principles

1. **Authenticity First** - Historical accuracy guides all design choices
2. **Emotional Impact** - Every visual element serves the story
3. **Clarity of Play** - Game mechanics remain clear despite artistic detail
4. **Cultural Respect** - Both factions portrayed with dignity and complexity
5. **Performance Balance** - Visual quality balanced with technical requirements

---

*This guide establishes the visual foundation for Ottoman Uprising, ensuring cohesive art direction across all game elements while maintaining historical authenticity and emotional resonance.*
