# Unit Visual Designs - Ottoman Uprising (Hero of Armenia)

## Unit Design Philosophy
**Historical Authenticity with Game-Play Clarity**
- Accurate period clothing and armor
- Distinct faction identification
- Clear visual hierarchy for gameplay
- Animation states that convey personality and status

---

## Armenian Units

### Hero Unit (Player Character)

**Visual Identity:**
- **Silhouette:** Athletic build, determined posture
- **Color Scheme:** Armenian red tunic, leather armor, stone gray cloak
- **Distinctive Features:** 
  - Traditional Armenian sword (similar to kinjal)
  - Embroidered cross on chest
  - Leather bracers with knotwork
  - Short cape for movement during stealth

**Animation States:**
- **Idle:** Alert stance, subtle breathing, occasional weapon check
- **Attack:** Fluid sword strikes, combination attacks, dodge rolls
- **Stealth:** Crouched movement, environmental awareness, shadow blending
- **Death:** Dramatic collapse, reaching for sky, final prayer gesture

**Visual Progression:**
- **Level 1-10:** Simple leather armor, basic sword
- **Level 11-20:** Chainmail reinforcement, decorated bracers
- **Level 21-30:** Full plate accents, ornate weapon
- **Level 31+:** Legendary armor with glowing runes, unique sword

### Allied Units

#### Armenian Warriors
**Visual Identity:**
- **Silhouette:** Strong, protective stance
- **Color Scheme:** Brown leather, red accents, stone gray
- **Distinctive Features:**
  - Round shields with Armenian cross
  - Short swords or spears
  - Chainmail hauberks
  - Conical helmets with nasal guards

**Variants:**
1. **Infantry:** Heavy armor, shield wall formation
2. **Archers:** Lighter armor, quivers, composite bows
3. **Cavalry:** Horse barding, lances, mounted archery
4. **Elite Guard:** Ornate armor, two-handed weapons

#### Armenian Villagers
**Visual Identity:**
- **Silhouette:** Varied, natural postures
- **Color Scheme:** Earth tones, natural fibers
- **Distinctive Features:**
  - Simple tunics and trousers
  - Agricultural tools as weapons
  - Head coverings for sun protection
  - Bare feet or simple sandals

**Variants:**
1. **Farmers:** Pitchforks, sickles
2. **Merchants:** Scales, carrying poles
3. **Crafts-tools:** Hammers, tongs
4. **Elders:** Staffs, robes

#### Religious Units
**Visual Identity:**
- **Silhouette:** Robed, dignified
- **Color Scheme:** Deep purple, gold, white
- **Distinctive Features:**
  - Clerical robes with ornate embroidery
  - Religious icons and crosses
  - Prayer books and censers
  - Staffs of office

**Variants:**
1. **Priests:** Healing abilities, morale boost
2. **Monks:** Combat training, defensive magic

## Turkish Units

### Khudatins (Elite Warriors)

**Visual Identity:**
- **Silhouette:** Tall, imposing, disciplined
- **Color Scheme:** Ottoman green, bronze, midnight blue
- **Distinctive Features:**
  - Lamellar armor with green lacquer
  - Curved swords (kilij or shamshir)
  - Round shields with Islamic geometric patterns
  - Turbans with metal plating

**Animation States:**
- **Idle:** Perfect military posture, occasional weapon cleaning
- **Attack:** Precise, deadly strikes, formation fighting
- **Death:** Falling with dignity, last stand gestures
- **Victory:** Disciplined celebration, religious gestures

**Visual Progression:**
- **Recruit:** Simple armor, basic weapons
- **Veteran:** Improved armor, decorated weapons
- **Elite:** Ornate armor, magical weapons
- **Champion:** Legendary equipment, glowing effects

### Bardouins (Heavy Cavalry)

**Visual Identity:**

**Animation States:**

**Variants:**

## Unit Distinction System

### Visual Hierarchy
1. **Size:** Larger units = more powerful
2. **Color Saturation:** More saturated = more important
3. **Detail Level:** More detail = higher tier
4. **Animation Complexity:** More complex = more valuable

### Faction Identification
**Armenian Units:**
- Predominantly red and green
- Cross symbolism
- Stone and leather textures
- Organic, flowing designs

**Turkish Units:**
- Predominantly green and blue
- Islamic geometric patterns
- Metal and silk textures
- Structured, symmetrical designs

### Status Indicators
- **Health Bars:** Above unit heads
- **Status Effects:** Glowing auras
- **Rank/Level:** Shoulder badges
- **Special Abilities:** Floating icons

---

## Animation State System

### Core States
1. **Idle:** Unit standing, breathing, looking around
2. **Move:** Walking, running, mounted movement
3. **Attack:** Primary attack animations
4. **Defend:** Blocking, dodging, parrying
5. **Death:** Final moments, collapse, disappearance
6. **Victory:** Celebration, morale boost

### Contextual States
1. **Stealth:** Crouched, hidden, environmental blending
2. **Interact:** Using objects, talking, trading
3. **Formation:** Group positioning, military drills
4. **Idle Variation:** Multiple idle animations for variety

### Animation Blending
- **Smooth Transitions:** 0.3-0.5 second blends
- **Interrupt Logic:** Important actions override lesser ones
- **Root Motion:** Movement integrated with animation
- **Procedural Elements:** Head tracking, weapon sway

## Visual Effect Integration

### Unit Effects
1. **Dust Particles:** Movement through environments
2. **Blood Effects:** Combat impacts
3. **Lighting:** Dynamic shadows and highlights
4. **Weather Interaction:** Rain, snow, wind effects

### Ability Effects
1. **Healing:** Green/white glow, particle streams
2. **Buff:** Blue/gold aura, ascending particles
3. **Debuff:** Red/purple aura, descending particles
4. **Ultimate:** Dramatic screen effects, unique animations

---

## Performance Considerations

### Level of Detail (LOD)
- **High Detail:** Within 20 meters, full animations
- **Medium Detail:** 20-50 meters, simplified animations
- **Low Detail:** 50+ meters, static models with movement
- **Far Detail:** 100+ meters, simple colored shapes

### Animation Optimization
- **Bone Count:** 50-100 bones per unit
- **Animation Clips:** 20-30 per unit type
- **Shared Animations:** Common actions reused
- **Procedural Animation:** Reduce clip count

---

*This unit design system creates distinct, memorable characters while maintaining historical authenticity and supporting gameplay clarity.*
