# Environment Designs - Ottoman Uprising (Hero of Armenia)

## Environment Design Philosophy
**Living History Through Space**
- Every location tells a story
- Environmental storytelling through detail
- Historical accuracy with dramatic license
- Varied gameplay opportunities in each setting

---

## Armenian Highlands

### Mountain Villages

**Mood Board Description:**
*"A cluster of stone houses cling to the mountainside like barnacles on a ship's hull. Smoke rises from clay-tiled roofs, mixing with morning mist. The scent of woodsmoke and bread mingles with mountain air. Somewhere, a church bell rings, its sound echoing off stone walls that have stood for centuries."*

**Visual Elements:**
- **Architecture:** Stone houses with wooden balconies
- **Roofing:** Red clay tiles, weathered with age
- **Streets:** Cobblestone paths, worn smooth by generations
- **Vegetation:** Grape vines, fig trees, herb gardens
- **Water:** Stone fountains, irrigation channels

**Lighting:**
- **Morning:** Golden light through mist, long shadows
- **Midday:** Harsh light, deep shadows in alleys
- **Evening:** Warm glow from windows, cooking fires
- **Night:** Moonlight, torchlight, starlight

**Sound Design:**
- Church bells, animal sounds, distant conversations
- Wind through trees, water in channels
- Smithy hammering, bread baking
- Children playing, elders storytelling

**Gameplay Integration:**
- **Stealth Opportunities:** Narrow alleys, rooftops
- **Combat Spaces:** Open squares, church yards
- **Social Areas:** Market squares, taverns
- **Verticality:** Multi-level buildings, cliff paths

### Highland Fortresses

**Mood Board Description:**
*"The fortress rises from the mountain like a natural extension of the rock itself. Its walls, built from the same gray stone, seem to grow from the cliff face. Arrow slits watch the valleys below, and the gatehouse looms like a granite guardian. Inside, courtyards echo with the sounds of military life."*

**Visual Elements:**
- **Walls:** Massive stone blocks, weathered by time
- **Towers:** Cylindrical and square, various heights
- **Gate:** Wooden doors reinforced with iron
- **Courtyards:** Multiple levels, connecting passages

**Lighting:**
- **Daytime:** Harsh shadows from battlements
- **Night:** Torches in sconces, moonlight on stone
- **Storm:** Dramatic lightning, rain on stone
- **Siege:** Fire glow, smoke effects

**Sound Design:**
- Wind through battlements, distant horns
- Guards' footsteps, armor clanking
- Gates opening/closing, chains rattling
- Battle preparations, weapons training

**Gameplay Integration:**
- **Vertical Combat:** Wall battles, tower assaults
- **Stealth Sections:** Night infiltration, guard patrols
- **Siege Mechanics:** Wall breaches, gate attacks
- **Defense Points:** Strategic positions, choke points

## Technical Considerations

### Level of Detail (LOD)
- **High Detail:** Player proximity, interactive elements
- **Medium Detail:** Visible but not interactive
- **Low Detail:** Distant scenery, atmospheric
- **Background:** Skybox, distant mountains

### Performance Optimization
- **Streaming:** Load areas as needed
- **Culling:** Hide non-visible geometry
- **LOD System:** Reduce detail with distance
- **Texture Management:** Mipmaps, atlasing

### Atmosphere Systems
- **Weather System:** Dynamic weather changes
- **Time of Day:** Lighting progression
- **Seasonal Changes:** Environmental variation
- **Fog of War:** Exploration mechanics
